# Code of conduct

## Our community

People of all backgrounds and experience levels can participate in IOFabrik project spaces. Treat others with respect. Discuss ideas and decisions without attacking people. Respect privacy and personal boundaries.

This policy applies to contributors and maintainers in project spaces and whenever they officially represent IOFabrik elsewhere.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, and personal attacks are unacceptable. Do not share someone's private information without consent or retaliate against anyone who reports a concern.

## Report a concern

Report concerns privately to [hello@iofabrik.com](mailto:hello@iofabrik.com). Describe what happened and include relevant links. Do not post sensitive reports in public issues.

An accused maintainer must not investigate the report or decide the outcome.

Report software vulnerabilities through the separate [security policy](SECURITY.md).

## How maintainers respond

Maintainers review reports fairly and share details only with people who need them to handle the report. They choose a response based on the severity of the behavior.

They may ask someone to correct their behavior, issue a warning, restrict participation temporarily, or remove them from the project. Serious incidents may lead to immediate removal.

To request reconsideration, email the same private contact. A maintainer who was not involved in the original decision will review the request where possible.

## Reference

This policy draws on [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) and uses project-specific wording.
