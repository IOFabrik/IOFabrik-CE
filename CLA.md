# IOFabrik Contributor License Agreement

> Draft 0.1 — for legal review only. Not an active agreement and not open for signature. Submitting a contribution or commenting on a change request does not constitute acceptance of this draft.

This draft is adapted from the [Harmony Combined Contributor Agreement Template, version 1.0](https://www.harmonyagreements.org/docs/ha-combined-v1), using the copyright-license alternative and outbound-license option five. It does not transfer copyright ownership.

The intended recipient is **IOFabrik, Netherlands**. Before activation, legal counsel must verify the recipient's exact legal identity and address (including the underlying person or entity if IOFabrik is a trading name), review the Dutch-law provisions, and approve the signing and record-retention process. Questions can be sent to [hello@iofabrik.com](mailto:hello@iofabrik.com).

## 1. Parties and scope

"We" and "Us" mean IOFabrik, the recipient identified in the final agreement. "You" means the individual contributor, or the legal entity identified by its authorized signatory, entering into that agreement.

"Contribution" means an original work of authorship that You intentionally submit for inclusion in IOFabrik's `IOFabrik-CE` project, including code, documentation, tests, and other project materials, to the extent You own or control the rights being granted.

"Material" means that project and the works incorporating Your Contribution. "Submit" includes communications through the project's source-control systems, issue tracker, or other channels managed by Us, but excludes material clearly marked "Not a Contribution." The "Submission Date" is the date of that submission.

"Copyright" means copyright and related rights in the Contribution that You own or control. The final agreement's "Effective Date" will be the date You explicitly accept its identified version through the approved signing process. Earlier contributions are covered only if specifically identified in that acceptance; future contributions are covered from the Effective Date.

## 2. Copyright ownership and license

You retain ownership of Your Contribution and may continue to use and license it Yourself. Rights not expressly granted remain Yours.

To the extent permitted by applicable law, You grant Us a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, transferable, royalty-free, irrevocable copyright license to reproduce, modify, display, perform, and distribute Your Contribution as part of the Material, and to sublicense those rights through multiple tiers of recipients. This grant is subject to the outbound-licensing commitment in section 4.

## 3. Patent license

For patent claims that You own, control, or have authority to license, You grant Us a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, transferable, royalty-free, irrevocable patent license, with rights to sublicense through multiple tiers of recipients, to make, have made, use, sell, offer for sale, import, and otherwise transfer Your Contribution and its combination with the Material.

This grant extends only to patent claims infringed by exercising those licensed rights in the Contribution or that combination. It is subject to section 4 and does not grant rights to unrelated inventions.

## 4. AGPL and alternative licensing

Based on the rights granted above, We may distribute and relicense Your Contribution as part of the Material under other terms, **including proprietary and commercial licenses**. This expressly permits distribution in a closed-source commercial edition without requiring further permission or royalty payments to You under this agreement.

As a condition of exercising that right, if We include Your Contribution in the Material, We will also license the Contribution under the public edition's [GNU Affero General Public License version 3.0](License.md). The public-license commitment applies to code and non-code contributions alike; this draft selects no separate media-license exception.

This agreement does not give Us ownership of Your Contribution, does not expand rights in third-party material, and does not itself grant recipients a commercial license to the project.

## 5. Other rights and use of contributions

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, You waive and agree not to assert moral rights in the Contribution against Us, Our successors, or recipients exercising the licensed rights. Any rights that cannot lawfully be waived remain unaffected. This provision requires specific review under Dutch law before activation.

We are not obligated to accept, include, distribute, support, or otherwise use any Contribution. No trademark rights are granted by this agreement.

## 6. Authority and third-party material

You confirm that You have legal authority to enter into the agreement and grant the stated copyright and patent rights. If signing for an entity, You must be authorized to bind that entity.

You confirm that the grants do not conflict with rights already granted to another party. If an employer or another person owns rights in the Contribution, obtain their authorization or arrange for the rights holder to enter into an appropriate agreement before submission. Contributors who cannot legally enter into the agreement must contact Us before signing.

Identify any material You do not own separately, provide its source and license, and disclose restrictions known to You, including relevant patent or trademark restrictions. Send authorization questions to [hello@iofabrik.com](mailto:hello@iofabrik.com). Do not represent third-party material as Your original work or imply that this agreement overrides its license.

Notify Us if You learn that these statements were materially inaccurate.

## 7. Disclaimer and liability

Except for the express representations in section 6, contributions are supplied as is, without additional warranties, to the extent permitted by applicable law. You are not required to provide support.

To the extent permitted by applicable law, neither party is liable under this agreement for lost profits, anticipated savings, data loss, or indirect, special, incidental, consequential, or exemplary damages. This clause does not exclude liability that cannot lawfully be excluded. Counsel must review these limitations before activation.

## 8. Governing law and general terms

The final agreement is intended to be governed by the laws of the Netherlands, excluding conflict-of-laws rules and the United Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods, to the extent such exclusions are permitted.

The executed agreement constitutes the parties' agreement concerning the rights it grants for covered Contributions. A transfer of rights or obligations requires the successor to agree in writing to the corresponding obligations, including section 4. Failure to enforce a provision on one occasion is not a continuing waiver. An unenforceable provision is to be limited or replaced to the extent legally permitted while preserving its purpose and the remainder of the agreement.

## 9. Signing status

Signing is **disabled** while this draft awaits legal review. Do not send signatures or identity documents, and do not use public comments as acceptance of this draft.

Before signing opens, IOFabrik must publish the approved agreement with its exact recipient identity, an immutable version reference, signing instructions, and information about collection, access, retention, and deletion of signing records. The process must record the signatory's identity, any entity authority, the agreement version, covered prior contributions if any, and explicit acceptance.

No automated CLA check is active yet. Maintainers must verify completion of the approved signing process before merging external contributions requiring a CLA.
